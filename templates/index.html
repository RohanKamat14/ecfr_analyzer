Overview

Welcome to the eCFR analyzer! currently we have 3 different options to view data.

Begin by selecting a search type from the dropdown!

Historical search: When you enter 2 dates in the form YYYY-MM-DD it will display the amount of historical changes between them.

Word count search: When you enter in the name of a institution (or select one from the dropdown) it will display the associated word count.

Corrections search: When you enter the number of a eCFR title it will display the amount of associated corrections.
